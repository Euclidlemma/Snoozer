# Snoozer privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Snoozer mutes your phone on a schedule you set. This policy explains what the app keeps and
where it goes. In short: **everything stays on your phone. Snoozer has no account, no ads and no
analytics, and it cannot connect to the internet.**

## What Snoozer stores, on your phone only

- **Your snoozers:** start and end times, days, category (Work or Study), on or off, and, if
  you give them, a name and first and last dates.
- **A log of quiet time**, for the weekly report: when each quiet stretch started and ended,
  its category, whether you turned the sound back up during it, and whether it couldn't run
  because Do Not Disturb access was off. Entries older than nine weeks are deleted
  automatically.
- **A few settings:** whether you've seen the introduction, the ringer mode to restore when
  quiet time ends, and whether alarms are set up.

## Timetable import

When you import a calendar file (.ics), Snoozer reads it on your phone. It keeps only each
class's name, times, days and dates, as snoozers. It doesn't keep the file or anything else
in it.

## What Snoozer sends

Nothing. Snoozer doesn't request internet access, so it can't send your data anywhere. It
collects no analytics or crash reports and shows no ads. Nothing is shared with the
developer or anyone else.

## Android backup

If you've turned on Android's own backup, Android may include Snoozer's snoozers and quiet-time
log in your phone's backup and restore them on a new phone. That backup is run by Android and
your Google account, not by Snoozer, and you control it in your phone's settings. The
introduction flag, the saved ringer mode and the alarm setup flag are left out of backups.

## Permissions

- **Do Not Disturb access:** to switch your phone to vibrate or silent and back.
- **Exact alarms:** to start and end quiet time on the minute.
- **Notifications:** for the reminder 5 minutes before quiet time starts.
- **Run at startup:** to set your schedule again after the phone restarts.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Snoozer deletes everything it stored. You can also delete single snoozers in
the app, or clear the app's storage in your phone's settings.

## Children

Snoozer isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anyone's personal
information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
